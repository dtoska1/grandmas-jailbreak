GrandmaPhone

Version 1.0.0 · by d

A Favorites-first Phone experience designed to keep everyday calling simple while preserving hidden family-maintenance access.

Grandma ModeAdminSuper AdminCall-aware

Grandma Mode

  • Phone opens and returns to Favorites.
  • Distracting Phone tabs and edit controls are hidden.
  • Home returns to Favorites.
  • App Switcher, Siri, Notification Center, and Control Center are blocked while unlocked.
  • Cellular and supported CallKit/WhatsApp calls remain usable.
  • After the final call ends, the phone returns to Favorites.

Admin modes

Normal Admin restores normal iPhone behavior temporarily and automatically expires after about 15 minutes.

Super Admin has no automatic timeout and remains active until deliberately exited.

Exact access gestures and internal implementation details are intentionally not published.

Compatibility

Physically tested on iPhone 7 · iOS 15.8.8 · Dopamine rootless · Sileo / ElleKit.
