GrandmaClock

Version 1.0.0 · by d

Readability-focused clock improvements without forcing huge global text sizes on unrelated apps.

Lock screenDateChargingStatus bar

Features

  • Larger lock-screen time.
  • Larger date and charging-status text.
  • Larger status-bar clock.
  • Dynamic width handling to prevent enlarged time strings from truncating.
  • No global text-size changes that would make names in apps such as WhatsApp unreadable.

Compatibility

Physically tested on iPhone 7 · iOS 15.8.8 · Dopamine rootless · Sileo / ElleKit.
