Grandma's Jailbreak

A rootless iOS 15 setup built around a simpler, more readable iPhone experience for Grandma.

GrandmaPhone
Simplified Phone/Favorites interface with Grandma Mode, temporary Admin modes, call-aware Home behavior, and automatic return to Favorites.
GrandmaClock
Larger status-bar and Lock Screen clock with clearer date and charging status.

Sileo source: https://dtoska1.github.io/grandmas-jailbreak/

The first 1.0.0 packages will be published after the exact release builds are physically validated on the tested iPhone 7.

Tested: iPhone 7 · iOS 15.8.8 · Dopamine rootless / ElleKit · package architecture iphoneos-arm64.
